Recount Text: Experiences of Helping Others

Recount text menceritakan kembali peristiwa atau pengalaman yang telah terjadi di masa lalu.

1. Orientation Memberitahu siapa, kapan, dan di mana cerita terjadi.
2. Events Urutan peristiwa atau tindakan yang dilakukan secara kronologis.
3. Reorientation Penutup cerita yang berisi perasaan atau kesan penulis.

Learn Simple Past Tense

Digunakan untuk menceritakan kejadian yang sudah terjadi di masa lampau.

Affirmative (+)

Subject + Verb 2 + Object/Complement

  • • I helped my friend yesterday.
  • • She visited her grandmother.
  • • They cleaned the classroom together.
Negative (-)

Subject + did not (didn't) + Verb 1

  • • I did not ignore my friend.
  • • She did not visit the park.
  • • They didn't leave the dirty room.
Interrogative (?)

Did + Subject + Verb 1 ... ?

  • • Did you help your friend?
  • • Did she visit her grandmother?
  • • Did they clean the classroom?

WH-Questions in Simple Past

What What did you do?
Who Who did you help?
Where Where did it happen?
When When did you visit?
Why Why did you help?
How How did you feel?

Teacher Corner & TPACK Framework

Tujuan Pembelajaran (TP)

  • Mengidentifikasi struktur Recount Text (Orientation, Events, Reorientation).
  • Mengidentifikasi & membuat kalimat Simple Past Tense (+, -, ?).
  • Memahami kosakata tindakan kepedulian sosial & ekspresi empati.
  • Menulis draf cerita pendek tentang pengalaman membantu orang lain (80-120 kata).
  • Mempresentasikan cerita secara lisan dengan percaya diri.

Langkah Pembelajaran (Deep Learning)

  1. Apersepsi: Diskusi awal tentang pengalaman membantu orang lain.
  2. Exploration: Membaca teks recount & mengeksplorasi kosakata care.
  3. Grammar & Listening: Belajar Simple Past Tense & menyimak dialog empati.
  4. Writing & Speaking: Membangun cerita dengan Story Builder & latihan bicara.
  5. Reflection & Assessment: Evaluasi kuis & pengiriman data ke Google Sheets.
